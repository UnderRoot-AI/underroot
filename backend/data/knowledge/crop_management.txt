Wheat (Gehun) grows best in cool weather (Rabi season, sown October-November). It needs a pH of 6 to 7.5, moderate nitrogen, and good drainage. It takes about 120 days to harvest. It is the main winter crop in north and central India.

Maize (Makka) is a Kharif crop sown in June-July. It grows fast and needs plenty of nitrogen and water especially when the plant is young. pH 5.8 to 7.5 is suitable. Harvest comes in 90 to 110 days.

Rice (Dhan/Chawal) needs a lot of water (flooded fields or frequent irrigation). It is grown in Kharif season. pH 5.5 to 7 is best. Takes 120 to 150 days. It is the main crop of eastern and southern India.

Groundnut (Moongfali) likes well-drained sandy-loam soil with pH 6 to 7. Too much water causes disease. Rabi or Kharif depending on region. Ready in 110 to 130 days.

Cotton (Kapas) grows in black cotton soil (Vertisols) and can handle alkaline soil up to pH 8. Needs warm temperature and water during growth. Takes 160 to 200 days. Kharif crop.

Potato (Aloo) prefers slightly acidic soil (pH 5 to 6.5) and cool temperature (15-20°C). Well-drained loose soil is best. Rabi crop. Ready in 80 to 110 days.

Chickpea (Chana) is a Rabi legume that fixes nitrogen from air. Grows well in light soils pH 6 to 8. Drought tolerant once established. Ready in 90 to 110 days.

Mustard (Sarson) is a Rabi oilseed crop. Good for alkaline soils, pH 6 to 8. Grows in 90 to 120 days. Needs cool temperature to start.

Soybean grows in Kharif season. Needs pH 6 to 7. Moderate water. Ready in 90 to 100 days. Good for soil nitrogen because it is a legume.

Bajra (Pearl Millet) is very drought tolerant. Grows in sandy soil with low rainfall. pH 6 to 7.5. Kharif crop. Ready in 70 to 90 days. Good for dry regions.

Jowar (Sorghum) is a Kharif crop tolerant of dry conditions. pH 6 to 7.5. Needs less water than maize or rice. Ready in 90 to 120 days.

When choosing a crop, think about: your soil test values, water availability, local market price, season, and what you have grown before (crop rotation is important to prevent disease buildup).
